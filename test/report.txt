Threat advisory: QakBot-style campaign targeting finance teams (TLP:CLEAR)

Summary
On 12 Oct 2026 the SOC observed phishing emails from billing@invoice-portal[.]example and
payments(at)secure-docs[.]top delivering a ZIP archive (Invoice_88213.zip) that contains
Invoice_88213.lnk. The shortcut runs mshta.exe against hxxps://cdn-update[.]top/load/x.hta and
then downloads a DLL from hxxp://185.220.101[.]45:8080/update.dat (Tor exit) and 45.155.205[.]233.
