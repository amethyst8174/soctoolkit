Tracer-Lens

Drop in a packet capture and read it as a dashboard instead of a packet list. Tracer-Lens charts protocols and traffic over time, maps flows and top talkers, enriches every external IP with offline geo, ASN, threat and VPN data, flags anomalies like beaconing and scans, and hands you hunt queries. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing is uploaded, looked up online or stored.

  • Parsed offline in your browser
  • The capture is never sent anywhere
  • Nothing is stored

Open a capture

Drop a .pcap or .pcapng here

Ethernet / IPv4 / IPv6 captures from Wireshark, tcpdump or similar. Large files are read in chunks; nothing is uploaded.

Reading…

Flows, not packets

Conversations, top talkers, protocol mix and a traffic timeline, with a host graph so the shape of the capture is visible at a glance.

Offline enrichment

Every external IP is matched against bundled geo, ASN, Tor, threat-list and VPN data. No reputation or geolocation service ever sees what you looked at.

Findings & hunts

Beaconing, scans, cleartext credentials, DGA-style DNS, SMBv1 and threat hits are flagged, and the IOCs become queries for Sentinel, Splunk, Elastic and CrowdStrike.

Why it's safe to open confidential captures here
  • Processed on your device. The file is read and parsed as code in your browser tab, in chunks, so even multi-gigabyte captures never leave your machine.
  • The browser blocks outbound traffic. A Content Security Policy forbids this page from sending requests or submitting forms, so data cannot leave even by mistake.
  • Enrichment is local. Geo, ASN, threat and VPN checks use lists shipped with the site, not online services.
  • Nothing persists. No accounts, cookies or browser storage. Leaving or reloading the page wipes everything.
  • Verify it yourself. Open DevTools: the Network tab stays empty while you work. The full source is on GitHub.
