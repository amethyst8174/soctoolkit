Timeline Merger

Timeline Merger

Paste or load logs from several systems, and get one clean, time-sorted timeline. It normalizes syslog, CEF, LEEF and JSON, converts every timestamp to the timezone you choose, and merges your sources together so you can read an incident across tools in order. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing is uploaded, looked up online or stored.

  • Parsing runs offline in your browser
  • Logs are never sent anywhere
  • Nothing is stored

Add log sources

Waiting for logs

Four formats, one shape

Syslog (RFC 3164 and 5424), CEF, LEEF and JSON lines are parsed into the same normalized event: time, source, host, severity and message, with the rest kept as fields.

Timezones reconciled

Timestamps with an offset are read as-is; naive ones (like BSD syslog) take the timezone you pick. Everything is sorted on absolute UTC, then shown in the zone you choose.

One ordered story

Several exports merge into a single sorted timeline, so you can follow an incident across firewall, EDR and app logs in the real order it happened.

Why it's safe to merge confidential logs here
  • Processed on your device. Parsing, timezone conversion and sorting all run as JavaScript in this tab. The logs you load are never uploaded to any service.
  • The browser blocks outbound traffic. A Content Security Policy forbids this page from sending requests or submitting forms, so data cannot leave even by mistake.
  • Nothing persists. No accounts, cookies or browser storage. Leaving or reloading the page wipes every source and the merged timeline.
  • Exports stay local. CSV and JSON of the timeline are built in your browser and saved straight to your device, never sent anywhere.
  • Verify it yourself. Open DevTools: the Network tab stays empty while you work. The full source is on GitHub.
