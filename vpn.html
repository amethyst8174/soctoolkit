VPN IP checker

Paste IP addresses, or any text that contains them, and see which belong to a VPN, Tor or iCloud Private Relay. Every answer shows its source and a confidence level, and anything not on a published list is reported as Unknown.

  • Lookups run offline in your browser
  • IPs are never sent anywhere

Waiting for IP addresses

Published lists only

Tor Project exit nodes, Apple's iCloud Private Relay ranges, Proton VPN and NordVPN server lists, and X4B's VPN and datacenter lists, refreshed twice a week.

Confidence on every answer

High when the operator publishes the address, Medium for neighbouring or curated VPN networks, Low for hosting networks, Unknown when no list names it.

No guesses

An address is never called a VPN without a listed source. Country and network owner come from offline registry data.

Why it's safe to check confidential IPs here
  • Processed on your device. The lists are part of this page. Checking an IP is a lookup in your browser, so no VPN, reputation or geolocation service ever sees it.
  • The browser blocks outbound traffic. A Content Security Policy forbids this page from sending requests or submitting forms, so data cannot leave even by mistake.
  • Nothing persists. No cookies or browser storage. Leaving or reloading the page wipes everything.
  • Verify it yourself. Open DevTools: the Network tab stays empty after you check. The full source is on GitHub.
