DecodeSphere

DecodeSphere

Paste an obfuscated PowerShell one-liner or a full process command line and get the real command back. It peels Base64 -EncodedCommand, gzip payloads, hex, character codes, caret and set-variable tricks one layer at a time, then flags the behaviour and pulls out the IOCs. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing is uploaded, looked up online or stored.

  • Decoding runs offline in your browser
  • Payloads are never sent anywhere
  • Nothing is stored

Paste a command

Waiting for input

Peels every layer

Obfuscation is stacked: a caret-escaped cmd wrapping a Base64 -enc wrapping a gzip blob. The decoder applies one transform at a time and shows the whole chain, so you see exactly how it was hidden.

Flags the behaviour

The decoded command is scanned for download cradles, IEX, reflection, LOLBins, Defender tampering and persistence, each marked High, Suspicious or Note.

Hands you the IOCs

URLs, IPs, domains, UNC and file paths and registry keys are pulled from the result and ready to copy straight into a hunt.

Why it's safe to decode confidential payloads here
  • Processed on your device. Every transform, including gzip decompression, runs as code in your browser tab. The command you paste is never uploaded to any service.
  • The browser blocks outbound traffic. A Content Security Policy forbids this page from sending requests or submitting forms, so data cannot leave even by mistake.
  • Nothing persists. No accounts, cookies or browser storage. Leaving or reloading the page wipes everything.
  • Decoding only. The tool reads and transforms text. It never runs, executes or reaches out to anything it decodes.
  • Verify it yourself. Open DevTools: the Network tab stays empty while you work. The full source is on GitHub.
