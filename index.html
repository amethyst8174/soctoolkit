SOC Toolkit

Free SOC utilities that are safe to use with confidential company data. Triage phishing, read email headers, check IPs for VPNs and turn threat reports into hunt queries. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing is uploaded, looked up online or stored.

  • Safe for confidential data
  • Nothing leaves your browser
  • No AI, no APIs, nothing stored

Built for confidential data

Nothing leaves your deviceEvery tool runs as code in your browser tab. There is no server, so emails, headers, logs and IPs are never uploaded.
Enforced by the browserA strict Content Security Policy blocks these pages from making any outbound request, so data cannot leave even by mistake.
No AI, APIs or live lookupsReputation, geolocation and VPN checks use lists shipped with the site. No third party ever sees what you check.
Nothing is keptNo accounts, cookies or browser storage. Closing or reloading the page wipes everything.

Verify it yourself: open DevTools and the Network tab stays empty while you work. The full source is on GitHub.
